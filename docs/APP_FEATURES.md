# محفظي · Mohafezy — App Features

> **منصة تحفيظ القرآن الكريم التي تربط الطلاب بأفضل المحفظين المعتمدين.**
> *The Quran-memorization platform that connects students with certified
> teachers — in person, at the mosque, or online.*

This document describes Mohafezy's features for **marketing, app-store
listings, investor decks, and the website**. It is written feature-first and
benefit-first. (For technical internals see
[SOFTWARE_DESIGN_AND_STRUCTURE.md](SOFTWARE_DESIGN_AND_STRUCTURE.md).)

---

## ✨ The One-Liner

**Mohafezy** brings together students who want to memorize and perfect their
Quran recitation with **certified, verified Quran teachers (محفّظين)** — letting
them book sessions that fit their life: face-to-face, at a nearby mosque, or
live online. Smart scheduling, secure payments, a built-in interactive Mushaf,
and progress tracking — all in one beautifully crafted Arabic-first app.

## 🌟 Headline Features

### 1. Find the Right Teacher — Nearby or Online
- **Map-based discovery** of teachers around you, with distance shown.
- **Rich teacher profiles**: certifications, ratings & reviews, specialties,
  session types, and pricing.
- **Verified & certified teachers only** — every teacher's credentials are
  reviewed and approved before they can accept students.
- **Trust badges** highlight verified, top-rated, and experienced teachers.

### 2. Flexible Session Types
Mohafezy supports the way real Quran learning happens:
- **In person** — one-on-one at an agreed location.
- **At the mosque** — sessions hosted at the teacher's mosque.
- **Online** — live sessions over the teacher's own video link (Zoom / Meet /
  Teams), with automatic meeting setup and reminders.

### 3. Smart, Conflict-Free Booking
- **Real-time availability** — book only the slots a teacher actually has open.
- **Multiple booking paths**: instant booking, request-first (teacher approves),
  or bundle/subscription booking.
- **Slot locking** prevents double-booking the same time.
- **Calendar & schedule views** for both students and teachers.

### 4. Bundles & Subscriptions
- Buy a **package of sessions** at a better rate.
- **Track remaining sessions** and validity at a glance.
- Auto-book your first session when you subscribe.

### 5. Secure Built-in Wallet & Payments
- **In-app wallet** with a transparent transaction history.
- **Top-up** your balance and **pay for sessions** in one tap.
- **Direct-payment flow** with teacher confirmation for added trust.
- **Promo codes** for discounts and campaigns.
- **Teacher payouts** — teachers withdraw earnings directly from their wallet.
- Designed to support local payment methods for the MENA market.

### 6. Interactive Quran Mushaf & Progress Tracking
- **Built-in Mushaf** with page-by-page navigation.
- Teachers can **mark mistakes directly on the page** during a session.
- Students **review their mistakes** on the exact ayah where they happened.
- **Memorization (حفظ) and revision (مراجعة) tracking** carries forward session
  to session, so progress is never lost.

### 7. Sessions, Quizzes & Challenges
- **Session completion reports** capturing what was memorized and revised.
- **Quizzes and challenges** teachers can set for students to reinforce learning.
- **Celebratory feedback** to keep students motivated.

### 8. Ratings, Reviews & Reputation
- Students **rate teachers** and leave reviews after sessions.
- Teacher ratings update automatically, building a **trusted reputation system**.

### 9. Real-time Notifications
- **Instant push notifications** for booking updates, acceptances, reminders,
  and payments.
- **Session reminders** before each session so no one misses a lesson.
- **In-app notification center** keeps everything in one place.

### 10. A Polished, Arabic-First Experience
- **Fully Arabic, right-to-left (RTL)** interface designed for the region.
- Elegant **teal-and-gold Islamic theme**.
- **Fast and offline-friendly** — the app works smoothly even on patchy
  connections thanks to local caching.

## 👩‍🎓 For Students — Feature Summary

- Discover certified teachers on an interactive map, filtered by distance.
- Browse detailed teacher profiles with ratings, reviews, and pricing.
- Book in-person, mosque, or online sessions around your schedule.
- Choose single sessions, bundles, or subscriptions.
- Pay securely with an in-app wallet, direct payment, or promo codes.
- View upcoming and past sessions, plus a personal schedule/calendar.
- Track memorization progress and review marked mistakes on the Mushaf.
- Earn rewards and take part in teacher-set challenges.
- Rate teachers and manage your profile, privacy, and location settings.

## 👨‍🏫 For Teachers (المحفّظون) — Feature Summary

- **Guided setup wizard** and credential upload for verification.
- **Manage your students** from a single dashboard.
- **Set your availability** and **define your own pricing** and bundles.
- Accept or decline **booking requests**; confirm direct payments.
- Run **in-person, mosque, or online** sessions with automatic meeting links.
- Record **session outcomes**, mark Quran mistakes, and set quizzes/challenges.
- Track **upcoming and completed sessions** and per-student progress.
- **Built-in wallet** with earnings tracking and **payout requests**.
- **Commission tiers** that reward higher-volume teachers.

## 🛡️ For Admins & Platform Operations

- **Teacher verification** — review and approve/reject credentials.
- **User management** — view, suspend, and manage accounts.
- **Broadcast notifications** to all users or by role.
- **Promo-code management**, **wallet top-up approvals**, and **payout processing**.
- **System settings & maintenance mode**, payment-event monitoring, and a full
  **audit log** of administrative actions.

---

## 🔒 Trust, Safety & Privacy

- **Verified teachers only** — credentials reviewed before going live.
- **Secure authentication** (email/password and Google Sign-In) with App
  Integrity protection.
- **Self-service account deletion** and **privacy controls** built in, in line
  with app-store data-safety requirements.
- **Financial records retained** appropriately for accounting/legal purposes,
  while personal data can be removed on request.
- **Clear cancellation and no-show policies** that protect both students and
  teachers.
